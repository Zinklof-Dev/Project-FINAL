	- UNDEAD -

	- Undead Soldier (Uncommon, Still stupid, Lightweight defense, Unable to use any weapons)

	- SIMPLE -
"Rogues, Thieves, Vigilantes, Shady Merchants, what exactly isn't out there that could potentially put a dagger through your chest?"
- Common & Innocent:
	- Townsfolk (Residents of towns you walk past. Little to no real protection or weapon efficiency, but can occasionally wield something that'll take you by surprise.)
		- Farmer (Pitchforks and torches make for great witch hunters who know nothing but grow crops and die of smallpox)
		- Cleric (Act as support for their allies without the need for magic. They'll run out of supplies eventually)
		- Priest (Support their allies through weak holy magic)
		- Blacksmith (Wield homemade weapons and lightweight yet lackluster armor)
		- Delinquints (Can sometimes carry items like tripwires, molotovs, etc. A nusiance for everyone if left unattended. Their tricks might backfire on their friends.)
	- Town Guardians (A significant step-up from the Townsfolk. Law enforcement occupy roles that they especially excel in)
		- Footman (Simple blade and blunt-using rookies with light armor. Typically accompanied by someone who ranks higher than them)
		- Archer (Wield standard-duty bows with a limited amount of arrows. Also equipped with a smaller side weapon to fall back to in case you're not dead by then)
