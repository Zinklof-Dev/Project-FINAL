	- SIMPLE -
"Rogues, Thieves, Vigilantes, Shady Merchants, what exactly isn't out there that could potentially put a dagger through your chest?"
- Common & Innocent:
	- Townsfolk (Residents of towns you walk past. Little to no real protection or weapon efficiency, but can occasionally wield something that'll take you by surprise.)
		- Farmer (Pitchforks and torches make for great witch hunters who know nothing but grow crops and die of smallpox)
		- Cleric (Act as support for their allies without the need for magic. They'll run out of supplies eventually)
		- Priest (Support their allies through weak holy magic)
		- Blacksmith (Wield homemade weapons and lightweight yet lackluster armor)
		- Delinquints (Carries items like tripwires, molotovs, etc. A nusiance for everyone if left unattended. Their tricks tend to also affect their friends. Not trained for combat or self defense)
	- Town Guardians (A significant step-up from the Townsfolk. Enemies start to occupy roles that they especially excel in)
		- Footman (Simple blade and blunt-using rookies with light armor. Typically accompanied by someone who ranks higher than them)
		- Archer (Wield standard-duty bows with a limited amount of arrows. Also equipped with a smaller side weapon to fall back to in case you're not dead by then)
		- Mercenary (Specialized soldiers meant to target specific groups or individuals that are just too much for footmen to handle. Excel with more valuable equipment and complicated builds)
		- Executioner (A heavy axe-wielding menace who deals MUCH more damage to characters with less health, whilst simultaneously targeting weaker characters to pick them off)
		- Ranger (A much more sophisticated (and legal) version of the Delinquint. Specializes with long-range combat and trap set-ups. Not very deadly while up close, but still capable of shoving someone away)
		- Knight (Much like the footman, but with more valuable weapons and protection. Knights with a one-handed weapon will use a shield in their free hand. Can occasionally be seen riding on the back of a horse)
			- Squire (Can be found accompanying a knight in their journeys. Acts like a footman in terms of behavior, but inheriting lesser equipment that is similar to the knight they are paired with)
		- Hunter (Very similar to the ranger, but with much less focus on traps, with that focus being shifted towards combat capabilities in both long-range and short-range. Additionally, they're accompanied by tamed wolves)
			- Hunting Wolf (Follows their owner closely, only leaving their side when told to attack a specified target. While fast and deadly, they lack endurance and armor piercing capabilities. Their behaviors are rather simple and predictable)
